Weekly Briefing Insights

Power Sector Round-up

Period: August 3rd Week (16/08 to 23/08) Compiled by: Vinay Pandey
Policy Insights

On 14th, to operationalise the SHANTI Act, 2025, Department of Atomic Energy released ‘Draft SHANTI Rules, 2026’ and the ‘Draft SHANTI Regulations 2026’. Suggestions & feedback on both may be mailed by 8:00 PM on 04 Sept.26 to feedback.shanti@dae.gov.in.

— DAE Notification
Legal Briefs

On 17th, Supreme Court, in the Karnataka Discom appeal (CA 8808/2026) against APTEL interim order allowing Adani Power's ₹1,005 Cr invoice, refused to grant Stay but directed APTEL to decide the Appeal in 3 months.

— Judicial Update

Days after Maharashtra CM letter to Prime Minister on pro IPP bias of APTEL, Solicitor General Tushar Mehta requested the top court: "The CJI must examine what is happening in APTEL". He was pursuing an urgent hearing on Appeal against APTEL's refusal to allow review by MERC in JSW Energy case.

— ET, Aug 22
Regulatory Frameworks

On 14th, CERC, using ‘Power to Relax’ in petition No. 5/SM/2026, prescribed a Milestone Extension Charge (MEC) framework for connectivity grantees seeking additional time to meet the Land, Financial Closure, and COD milestones prescribed under GNA Regulations.

— CERC Order
Commercial Deal Flow

Oriana Power has won a ₹214.80 Cr EPC contract from a leading cement player to develop 50 MW of solar power projects in Rajasthan. The project timeline is 9 months and includes 10-year O&M.

— Corporate Release

MoP is exploring a proposal to provide about ₹18,700 Cr subordinated debt at a low interest rate for NHPC’s 1,856 MW Sawalkote HEP in J&K. Last year, an MNRE expert committee estimated the project cost at ₹31,380 Cr.

— ET, Aug 19

In SECI's 6,000 MWh (1,500 MW x 4 hrs) Assured Peak Power tender, Waaree Energies won 700 MW at a tariff of Rs. 5.99/kWh, followed by NTPC Renewables and Acme Solar Holdings securing 500 MW & 750 MW, respectively, at Rs. 6/kWh.

— Saur Energy, Aug 22

Hindustan Power has achieved financial closure with IREDA for a debt of ₹1,135 Cr. The 435 MWp solar power project in Lalitpur, won in 2025, has a 25-year PPA with UPPCL.

— Financial Closure Record

On 21st, Power Grid won LOI to integrate 5,500 MW from Jam Khambhaliya Ph II and 1,000 MW from Jamnagar PH I under TBCB (BOOT model) at an annual transmission charge of ₹822.91 Cr. Scope includes building a new GIS, four 1,500 MVA & ten 500 MVA transformers, 765 kV DC lines, and other augmentations.

—TBCB Win

As per unconfirmed reports, Power Grid has also won a ₹26,000 Cr mega contract. Scope includes 6,000 MW terminal stations at Barmer-II & South Kalamb, Maharashtra with about 900 km ±800 kV HVDC line, a new pooling S/s at Barmer-II with connecting line to Barmer-I, and other extensions.

— T&D India, Aug 23
