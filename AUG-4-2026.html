Weekly Briefing Insights

Power Sector Round-up

Period: August 4th Week (24/08 to 29/08) Compiled by: Vinay Pandey
Policy Insights

MNRE is exploring changes to the supply profile of stalled RE projects, including the addition of BESS, to help nearly 42 GW of auctioned capacity find buyers.

— Fin Exp, Aug 24

The Department of Atomic Energy is planning a new consulting subsidiary of NPCIL which will offer Design, Engineering & Regulatory support for PHWR units to private companies going nuclear.

— ET, Aug 27

On August 27th, MoP clarified that RE projects can use curtailed T-GNA power to charge additional co-located BESS (not covered under PPA) and sell such power without an NoC from the Procurer.

— Bus. Stand, Aug 29
Legal Briefs

The Singapore International Commercial Court rejected Tata Power's challenge to a $490 million arbitration award for violating an agreement with Kleros Capital to bid for a coal asset in Russia, thereby denying it the opportunity to benefit.

— Bus. Stand., Aug 27
Regulatory Frameworks

On August 19th, NLDC issued a comprehensive Operating Procedure 2026 aligning with IEGC 2023. The 345-page document mandates an IRP, PRAS/SRAS & TRAS framework, System restoration & Grid defence mechanisms (including frequency relays & cyber security), alongside a detailed procedure for ISTS T-GNA.

— Regulatory Release

Tata Power has published a Public Notice on its application before the Chhattisgarh SERC (petition no 89 of 2026) for a Distribution License in Raipur, Dharsiwa, Mandir Hasoud, and Abhanpur Tehsils of the Raipur District. Comments or objections may be submitted within 30 days to the Secretary, CSERC. The Public Hearing is scheduled for 01/10/26.

— Public Notice
Commercial Deal Flow

Macquarie Asset Management and the RP Sanjiv Goenka Group’s Purvah Green have emerged as the final two contenders to acquire Welspun New Energy Ltd at an enterprise value of approximately ₹2,000 Cr.

— ET, Aug 24

According to industry reports, the Aditya Birla Group is in advanced talks with domestic lenders for a ₹14,000+ Cr debt package to finance its acquisition of Shell’s Indian RE platform, Sprng Energy.

— Market Reports

On August 25th, L&T informed that its RE vertical secured a major order valued between ₹5,000 and ₹10,000 Cr to develop 3 BESS projects boasting a 6 GWHr (1.5GW x 4) capacity in the Middle East. The project scope encompasses grid interconnections, pooling substations, and underground cabling.

— L&T Disclosure, Aug 25

Suzlon has secured a new 250MW wind project order consisting of 76 units of its 3.3 MW S144 model from Torrent Green. This marks the 6th agreement between the two entities, bringing their cumulative total beyond 1.3 GW.

— Yahoo Fin, Aug 26
