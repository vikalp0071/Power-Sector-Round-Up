Account & Data Deletion Request

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy, users of CSPC Inventory have the right to request deletion of their user accounts and associated authentication credentials.

Instant Deletion: You can delete your account immediately directly inside the app without submitting an email request.

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Immediate)

  1. Open the CSPC Inventory app on your Android device.
  2. Ensure you are signed in with your corporate credentials.
  3. Tap the top-right Menu ▼ dropdown.
  4. Select Delete Account (highlighted in red).
  5. Review the confirmation dialog and tap Delete Cognito Account.

Your profile and tokens are immediately purged from AWS Cognito User Pools, and local tokens are cleared.

Method 2: Remote Deletion Request via Email

If you uninstalled the app or lost device access, email the administrator to request manual removal:

  • Send an email from your registered corporate address to: vikalp.tiwari@cspc.co.in
  • Subject line: [Account Deletion Request] - CSPC Inventory
  • Include your Corporate User ID and Assigned Store Code.
  • Requests are verified and processed within 48 business hours.

Data Retention vs. Deletion Details

Data Type Action Taken Retention Rationale
Login Credentials & Passwords Permanently Deleted Purged completely from AWS Cognito User Pool immediately upon deletion.
Local Session Tokens & Cache Permanently Cleared Wiped from device SharedPreferences.
Store Inventory & Diary Entries Retained Preserved in enterprise DynamoDB tables to satisfy mandatory statutory and thermal power plant auditing requirements.
Audit Trail Log Recorded A final "Account Deleted / Deregistered" entry is recorded in the 10-item plant audit trail for compliance verification.
