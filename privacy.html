Privacy Policy

Application: CSPC Inventory  |  Effective Date: January 1, 2026  |  Enterprise Internal Use

This Privacy Policy governs how CSPC Inventory collects, utilizes, retains, and protects personal and operational information collected from authorized corporate employees.

1. Information We Collect

To provide secure authentication and maintain verified power plant inventory audits, the application collects:

  • Corporate Email Address: Used solely as the unique identifier via AWS Cognito authentication (restricted to verified @cspc.co.in domain accounts).
  • Assigned Plant Store Code: Designates jurisdiction across power station substores (e.g., W62, X05).
  • Operational Logs: Timestamps, bin item adjustments, requisition updates, and transaction remarks recorded under the store audit trail.

2. How Information Is Used

All data collected is used strictly to provide functionality within designated plant substores:

  • Verifying identity and administrative privileges.
  • Preventing unauthorized updates to power station inventory records.
  • Maintaining a 10-record FIFO ring-buffer audit trail of inventory mutations and store access.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not share, sell, or disclose personal or operational data to any third parties or advertisers. Data is stored securely in dedicated AWS cloud infrastructure (AWS Cognito, AWS DynamoDB) within high-compliance data regions enforcing encryption at rest and in transit (TLS 1.3).

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Users can permanently delete their credentials and personal identification from AWS Cognito directly inside the app by selecting Menu > Delete Account or by submitting an online request via our Account Deletion Portal.

Historical store transaction logs and material consumption entries generated by the user are retained within the local plant store database for regulatory and enterprise auditing compliance.

5. Security Practices

  • All network transmissions use HTTPS / TLS 1.3.
  • Cleartext network traffic is explicitly disabled.
  • Passphrase re-confirmation is strictly enforced for destructive store mutations and diary record deletions.
