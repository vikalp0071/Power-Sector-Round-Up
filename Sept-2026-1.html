Power Sector Round up

September 1st Week
(30/08 to 05/09)

Policy

  1. In a reply to a Assembly question, Haryana Energy Minister said that the state govt continues to oppose proposal for parallel distribution licence in Gurugram & Nuh. He also said that govt would approach the courts if necessary. (HT, Sept 01)
  2. After Gujrat & Tamil Nadu Tender flop, India is seeking advice from UK Netherlands, & European experts to restructure its offshore wind strategy. High upfront costs (about 4 times), tariff uncertainty, transmission readiness are some of the factors deterring developers and investors (ToI, Sept 02)
  3. A CEA panel to examine potential damage to thermal plants from flexible operations has recommended two shift operation of 151 old coal based units. To avoid consecutive ramp-up and ramp-down of same units; advises selection of group of units for ramp-up and ramp-down. (ToI, Sept 01)
  4. During a stakeholder consultation by NITI Aayog, Private players sought green energy status for nuclear power to enable access to green bonds, green loans and blended financing for nuclear projects. A clarification was also requested to settle infrastructure status of nuclear power projects. (Ind. Exp. Sept 02)

Legal

  1. Rejecting Slum dweller’s claim under SRA scheme, Bombay High Court, has held that every commercial electricity bill cannot be treated as proof of commercial eligibility.

Regulatory

  1. Draft 2nd Amend. to CEA’s Tech. Standards for Const. of Plants & Lines Reg. proposes all PCS of BESS commissioned after 01.07.27 to have grid forming control & min 15% inverter with grid forming control for RE plants. Also Ground Soal & onshore wind will need collocated ESS of min 10% cap. for 2 Hrs, (for COD after 01.07.30 – 4hrs). Comment by 04.10.26.

Commercial

  1. MSEDCL MD Lokesh Chandra has claimed that Co. is financially comfortable after spinoff of MSEB Solar Agro Power Ltd. (Bus. Stand, Sept 01). However, experts point out that ideally, MSEDCL must pay charges to the new entity, on all the T&D assets created previously through agricultural grants / subsidies.
  2. Coal India is investing nearly fifty thousand Cr in coal gasification projects. It is also actively seeking overseas critical mineral assets. CILis also targeting 9.5 GW of RE capacity by 2030.
  3. SAEL has secured orders for 1 GWp of solar PV modules (N-type TOP Con) over past six months. 585.8 MWp from NTPC REL’s Chitrakoot solar project forms largest part of order book. (Business today, Sept 03)
  4. POWERGRID has won a BOOT based LoI of ₹3,244.33 Cr Annual Trans. charges for evacuating 6 GW RE power from Barmer-II solar projects. Scope include terminals at Barmer-II and South Kalamb, Approx. 1,000-km ±800 kV HVDC line and associated systems (ET, Rajasthan, Sept 04)
  5. Inox Wind has won a repeat 100 MW EPC wind power project order from Indian Oil. The ₹755 Cr contract also cover 10 yr O&M. (SAHI, Sept 03)
  6. Ceigall India has won LoI worth approx. ₹5,300 Cr (Annual Trans. charge of ₹608.67 Cr) to develop a 765/400 kV AIS with about 300 km lines for power evacuation from Lakadia (Ph II: 7.5GW), Jam Khambhaliya (Ph-II: 5.5GW) & Jamnagar (Ph-I: 1GW) - Part-B. (Bus. Stand, Sept 03)
  7. On Sept 03, PGCIL received LOI under TBCB for a BOOT basis ISTS to integrate 7.5 GW RE assets in Lakadia REZ (Phase II), at Annual Transmission Charge of Rs. 1,152.49 Cr. The scope entails a new 765/400kV Lakadia-II sub-station, 765kV lines across the state, & commissioning of a synchronous condenser.
  8. Aditya Birla Group has launched its wires and cables business, “Ultravolt” (housed under UltraTech Cement). Backed by an investment of Rs.1,800 Cr, by capacity, it will be 2nd largest player, in the wires segment. (ET, Sept 03)
  9. Goenka’s Purvah Green is acquiring Welspun New Energy for approx. ₹2,500 Cr. Welspun New operates 45 MW RE capacity with pipeline targeting 5 GW RE & 2 MTPA of green derivatives by 2030. (SAHI, Sept 04)
  10. NLC India Renewable has got LoI form GUVNL for 275 MW/550 MWh standalone BESS. (Solar Quarter, Sept 05)
  11. Due to loss of about 360-550 MW hydro capacity in recent flash floods, Nepal has requested imports from Sept. itself (usually it’s from start of winter in Oct) (The Hindu, Aug 29)

Statistics

  1. Driven by a 12.85% jump in total energy consumption, in Aug. 26, IEX reported its highest ever monthly trade vol. of 13.94 BU (20.2% YoY increase). DAM ACP rose 22% (YoY) to ₹4.88 / unit, & RTC rose by 30% to Rs 4.41/ unit but REC trade fell by 86.6%. (ET, Sept 03)

Initiatives

  1. With CoD of 358 MWac solar plant & 1.2 GWh BESS, the world’s largest solar-storage microgrid goes online in Saudi Arabia. It will supply power to an isolated network covering tourism facilities, desalination, cooling, water treatment and other infra. (PV Magazine, Sept 01)

Concern

  1. During April-July FY27, while about 8,236 MWh BESS & 250 MW PSP achieved CoD, on YoY basis, ESS tendering & awards dropped 26% and 65% respectively. Further, the commercial cell manufacturing remains limited, leaving the domestic BESS industry dependent on imported cells. (Fin. Exp., Aug 29)
